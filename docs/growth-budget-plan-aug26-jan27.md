# PART B — STANDING ROUTINES (do these without being told, all 26 weeks)

## B1. Daily, 15 minutes, every morning at 9:00
1. Open the metrics Google Sheet (you build it in Week 1, instructions there). Fill yesterday's row: installs (Play Console + App Store Connect once iOS is live), orders, GMV, orders per promo code, ad spend (Meta Ads Manager).
2. Reply to every customer WhatsApp message from overnight.
3. Check stock levels of top 10 SKUs; if any is below 3 units, message the brand for a consignment top-up today.

## B2. Daily, whenever an order is delivered
1. Every order bag contains: the rating card insert + a small card that says: *"Loved it? Rate us on the Play Store (or App Store, if you're on iPhone) and get ₹50 off your next order — reply RATED on WhatsApp with a screenshot."* (You print these in Week 1; App Store isn't live yet at print time, but the card text works for both from day one.)
2. 24 hours after delivery, send the customer this WhatsApp: *"Hey [name], Adi from Cosmo here. How was the [top item in order]? One line of honest feedback helps me pick what to stock next."* — this drives repeat rate more than any ad.

## B3. Weekly, every Sunday night, 45 minutes
1. Total the week in the metrics sheet. Compare against that week's goal in Part C. Write one line: MET or MISSED and why.
2. In Meta Ads Manager: sort ad sets by cost-per-install. Turn OFF the worst performer if it's >1.5× the best. Duplicate the best with +20% budget. Never touch anything mid-week.
3. Send the "Shelf Drop" broadcast (template in Week 4) to the full customer WhatsApp list: what's new this week + one Cosmo Score story.
4. Compute 14-day repeat rate for the cohort from two weeks ago (customers who first ordered then ÷ how many ordered again within 14 days).

## B3a. Monthly influencer cycle (₹15,000/month, every month)
1. First week of the month: book 2–3 nano collabs at ≤₹5k each (repeat the best performers; replace anyone below 15 installs/collab — check their code in the sheet).
2. Every deal includes: 1 reel + 1 story, their personal promo code, and ad usage rights.
3. When a collab clears 40 installs, push that video into Meta as an ad the same week — it's proven creative.

## B4. Fortnightly, alternate Fridays
Run the kill-criteria review (Part D). Every channel gets one word in the sheet: KEEP / KILL / SCALE.

# CONTENT FORMAT BANK — faceless only (Adi does not appear on camera, ever)

Every ad, reel, and post in this plan is made from these formats. None requires filming a person's face or talking to a camera. Rotate them; when one wins in ads, make 3 variants of it.

1. **Score Reveal** — product on plain surface (window light, sage-green cloth), animated 0–10 counter lands on its score, 3 text-overlay reasons. The signature Cosmo format.
2. **App scroll** — screen-record the app browsing scores; AI voiceover (ElevenLabs) or just captions + trending audio.
3. **Label Teardown** — macro shot of a mainstream snack's ingredient label, red circles appear on the bad ingredients with text callouts, ends "This scored 2/10. Here's what scored 9." + app CTA.
4. **This vs That** — two similar products side by side, scores appear above each, one-line why. (Score competitor-category products generically; don't name-and-shame brands you might partner with.)
5. **Hands-only unboxing** — a Cosmo order bag opened on a table, hands only, each product's score card revealed as it comes out.

7. **AI avatar founder** — if a "founder story" ad is ever worth testing, use a HeyGen/Synthesia avatar or an AI-voiced animated version — clearly stylised, never pretending to be real footage of Adi. Optional; test only if formats 1–3 plateau.

9. **Shelf restock timelapse** — the home shelf being restocked, text overlay: "This week's shelf, scored and stocked."

**Statics / carousels** (the nightly Instagram pipeline already handles carousels — feed it these angles)
10. Product + score badge + one savage-honest line from its rating explanation.
